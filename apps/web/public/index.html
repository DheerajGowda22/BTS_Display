Booth setup

  1. On the Staff desk, upload the jobs Excel file.
  2. Open TV display on the laptop driving the TV and press Full screen. Its QR code sends visitors' phones to the sign-up.
  3. Open Booth tablet on the tablet, press Full screen, mount it at the front of the booth. Staff leave it with Exit, top right.
  4. Before doors open on day 1, press Clear test entries on the Staff desk so rehearsal entries don't count.
